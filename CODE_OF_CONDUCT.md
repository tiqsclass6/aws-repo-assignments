# 🧭 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of the **aws-repo-assignments** project, pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Disability
- Ethnicity
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual identity and orientation

We are committed to fostering an open, inclusive, and professional environment for all participants.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive and productive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy, respect, and professionalism
- Being considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Prioritizing collaboration and project success

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or inappropriate jokes
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal, political, or discriminatory attacks
- Harassment (public or private)
- Publishing others’ private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional or inappropriate in an academic or workplace setting

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, or offensive.

Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories, issues, and pull requests
- Discussions, reviews, and comments
- Any external communication or community space referencing this project

This policy applies both within project spaces and when an individual is representing the project externally.

---

## Enforcement

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when responding to violations:

1. **Correction**  
   A private message addressing the behavior and expectations.

2. **Warning**  
   A formal written warning outlining the violation and required corrective action.

3. **Temporary Restriction**  
   Temporary limitation of participation (e.g., commenting, submitting issues or pull requests).

4. **Permanent Ban**  
   Permanent removal from all project participation for severe or repeated violations.
